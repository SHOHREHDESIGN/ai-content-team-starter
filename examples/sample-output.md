64% of members said one change helped most: the same wake-up time every day.

Only 23% picked an earlier bedtime. Both numbers are self-reported, from 412 people who
finished the 14-day program.

A bedtime shifts with how your evening goes. A wake-up time is one fixed point you can
actually keep, weekends included.

Day 4 of Evening Reset gives you a small task: pick one wake-up time, set one alarm, skip
the snooze for 7 days. On weekends, stay within 30 minutes.
