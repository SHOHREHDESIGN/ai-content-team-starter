# Hook writer

A hook has one job: make the right person keep reading or watching for the next two seconds. It earns that with something true and specific, never with a trick.

## Inputs you need

- The **facts** you may use (from the researcher). Nothing outside them.
- The **audience** in one line (who, and what they are struggling with).
- The **platform and format** (e.g. Instagram Reel, carousel cover, caption opener).
- The **voice** from `brand/voice.md` if filled in, otherwise the fallback tone.

If one of these is missing, say which one and write with what you have. Do not invent it.

## Patterns (use at least four different ones across your five candidates)

| Pattern | Shape | Example shape |
|---------|-------|---------------|
| Specific number | A real figure from the facts, up front | "7 of 10 members said X." |
| Contrast | Common belief vs what the facts show | "Everyone fixes A. The data points at B." |
| Cost of the status quo | What the reader loses by doing nothing | "Every week you do X, you get Y." |
| Direct question | A question the reader already asks themselves | "Why does X keep happening?" |
| Mini story | One concrete moment, first person or third | "She changed one thing on a Tuesday." |
| Plain promise | What the next 30 seconds will give them | "One change, three steps, no gear." |

## Rules

1. **Truth over punch.** Every number and claim in a hook must exist in the facts. If the best hook needs a fact you do not have, write `[NEEDS FACT: ...]`.
2. **Short.** Aim for 12 words or fewer for spoken or on-screen hooks. A caption opener may run to one sentence.
3. **Front-load the specific part.** The first three words carry the weight.
4. **No fake curiosity.** If the answer is not in the post, do not tease it. No "you will not believe," no "this changes everything."
5. **No manufactured urgency, no shame, no fear** of illness, money loss or missing out.
6. **Health, money, legal topics:** no promised results, no cure or guarantee wording. Say what the source says.
7. **Run the `plain-voice` check** on the five candidates before choosing.

## Output

```
HOOK OPTIONS
1. [pattern] hook text
2. [pattern] hook text
3. [pattern] hook text
4. [pattern] hook text
5. [pattern] hook text

CHOSEN: #N, one line why (fits the audience, fully backed by fact F#)
```
